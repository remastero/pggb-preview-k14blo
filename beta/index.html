PGGB V9 beta (9.1.48) for testers

Downloads, what is new since 9.1.47, and notes for testing.

The beta of PGGB V9, version 9.1.48, is ready for testing. Since 9.1.47 it adds the Library, on by default, where you browse, search and play your music, with History, playlists and backups. The new Dynamic Range Expander repairs clipping and undoes limiting. PGGB’s window can now show and control PGGB on another computer (Remote mode), and the Remote on your phone, tablet or browser gains the Library and more of the app’s pages. The fixes are listed below.

This is a beta. Some parts are still being finished. Please tell us what you see, even small things; Reporting a problem below says how.

Installation

  • Windows: this installer is not signed yet, so Windows may warn when you start it: choose More info, then Run anyway. On a PC with Smart App Control on, Windows will not start it until the signed installer replaces it on this page. The signed one installs the same PGGB, so you will not need to install again.
  • Mac: use the Apple silicon installer on M1 and later Macs and the Intel installer on Intel Macs. About This Mac in the Apple menu shows which chip yours has.
  • PAL endpoints: keep the ones you have; the beta needs no update.

What is new since 9.1.47

The Library

The Library is new, and it is on by default.

Details
  • Browse your music by album, artist, genre, work, track or folder, and search it. Each artist and genre has its own page.
  • Play an album, a folder or a track from its menu (right-click it, or tap ... on a touch screen): Play now, Play next, Add to queue or Shuffle.
  • Folders shows your music folders: sort them, show favourites only, list the tracks in a folder and its subfolders, or use Refresh to scan a folder for changes. An album’s folder opens as its tracks, all selected and ready to play.
  • Add to Offline in a folder’s menu adds it to Offline’s input folders, and PGGB tells you if it is already there.
  • Mark favourites with the heart. File info in a track’s menu shows the file’s name and full path.
  • Fetch an album’s details from MusicBrainz, with Undo and Name the release.
  • Choose the columns Tracks and Works show, and change the Library’s views with Customize views.
  • The Library’s player bar has Shuffle and Repeat (all, then one) for the play queue, also on the Remote in a computer’s browser.
  • The Play queue (the queue button in the player bar) shows what plays next, with Add files, Add folder, Load queue, Save queue and Clear queue. Right-click an entry, or tap ... on a touch screen, for Remove from queue, Move earlier or Move later, in the app and on the Remote.

Dynamic Range Expander and DR

The Dynamic Range Expander is new, on its own settings page, and off by default. It repairs clipped peaks and undoes limiting, in Live and, for PCM tracks, in Offline with Precision optimized processing.

Details
  • Clipping repair has Full and Lite. Always de-limit undoes limiting on more tracks; a track with clipping gets only the clipping repair. DSD tracks get both repairs in Live with Allow expansion of DSD tracks on, and play with the Alternate HF filter when Minimal is chosen; Always de-limit does not apply to them yet.
  • The status line says what the Expander does on each track, and a DR badge in the player shows what it did on the playing track. The badge appears only when a track needs it; hover over it for the details.
  • The Library shows each track’s DR (dynamic range), for example DR7 → 9 where the Expander changed it, and you can sort by it. History shows each play’s DR with what the Expander did, in short: for example DR7 → 9 CL(F), where C is clipping repaired, L limiting undone, and (F) or (L) the Full or Lite repair. Hover over it, or tap it on a touch screen, for the details. An album’s tracks show the same letters under their DR, from each track’s latest play. DSD tracks get their DR too: the Library measures them in the background, about a minute for a five-minute DSD64 track.

Sound and the player bar

Channels (Settings > Personalize Sound > Channels, also on the Remote): Swap channels, Invert polarity and Balance (up to 24 dB either way, not in Bypass), for Live. Files processed in Offline are not changed.

Details
  • In the player bar, Expander, EQ and Crossfeed are now under one DSP button, in the app and on the Remote. It sits where the clipping light was and turns red when Live clips.

Stealth

Stealth (in the header) pauses the Library’s automatic scans and lookups, records no plays and stops every log PGGB writes. Your logging settings are kept for when you turn it off.

History, playlists and tags

History lists your plays: when you played each track and how long you heard it. Filter it, search or sort by any column, or clear it.

Details
  • Albums show how many times you have played them.
  • Make, rename and delete playlists, and add albums, folders or tracks to them. Show a playlist in any of 13 orders; Shuffled shuffles again each time you choose it.
  • Make tags, and add albums or folders to them.

Library settings and backups

Set the Library’s text size and spacing in its settings, under Text and spacing.

Details
  • Backups: choose a folder, then back up the Library now or every day at a set time, and choose how many backups to keep. Restore checks a backup first, asks where your music is now if its folders have moved, and asks you to confirm.

Remote mode and the Remote

Remote mode: PGGB’s window can now show and control PGGB running on another computer on your network. Its Library, settings, queue and track controls work as they do in the app.

Details
  • The Remote now has the Library, with the app’s menus and pages for playing, History, playlists, tags, File info, MusicBrainz, Customize views and Backups.
  • Folders on the Remote works as in the app, with Sort by, Show favourites only, Tracks including subfolders and Refresh.
  • The Remote’s settings pages now work like the app’s. Find a setting finds settings and the pages’ buttons, such as Calibrate and Scan devices.
  • On the Remote, Offline’s Silence and Processing logs pages and the DAC pages work as in the app, and the DAC Selector applies your choice as soon as you click it.
  • On the Remote, the PCM and DSD rate limits are now tabs on every screen (they wrap on a phone), and the DSD modulator tabs read as in the app.
  • On the Remote, Help in the About pane now opens PGGB’s own help pages, laid out for a phone, instead of the online guide. They do not cover the Library or Remote mode yet.

Running in the background

PGGB now keeps running when you close its window, so music, the Remote and the Roon and UPnP inputs keep working. It is on by default: Settings > Application > Start and close > Keep PGGB running in the background when its window is closed.

Details
  • To open or quit PGGB, use its icon in the Windows notification area or the Mac’s menu bar; on a Mac you can also click PGGB in the Dock, or press Command-Q to quit. Starting PGGB again opens the one already running.
  • Start PGGB when you sign in (Open at login on a Mac) starts it in the background.

The waveform

A waveform pill shows the playing track’s waveform, in the app and on the Remote. It opens by default and stays closed once you close it; its gear sets how see-through it is, and its pin pins it.

Updates

Check for updates (Settings > Application > Updates) is off by default. When it is on, PGGB checks when it starts and once a day, and shows a newer version, what is new in it and a download link, in the app and on the Remote; it never installs anything itself.

Notes for testing

  • Refresh waits while Live is playing, while Offline is converting, while calibration runs, or while Stealth is on; the Folders page says which.
  • Please try starting at sign-in: turn on Start PGGB when you sign in (Open at login on a Mac), then sign out and back in (log out on a Mac). PGGB should start in the background, with its icon in the notification area (in the menu bar and the Dock on a Mac) and no window. Also sign out while PGGB runs in the background: your settings should be kept. Tell us if either doesn’t happen.
  • Check for updates looks at public releases only, so it shows nothing until a public release newer than 9.1.48 is out.
  • Expansion of DSD tracks is early: it works in Live only, not in Offline, and Always de-limit does not apply to DSD tracks yet. The Library shows a DSD track’s DR, but not yet the → of what the Expander changed.
  • Switching between Light and Dark while the Library is open can leave a few of its labels faint or blank, such as Focus on an album’s page. Restarting PGGB draws them again.

Fixed since 9.1.47

If you saw any of these, they are fixed now:

The 9 fixes
  • Auto Pass-through processed music from the PGGB Bridge (for example from Roon) even when its rate matched the output. It now passes it through untouched when the output is set to 32-bit.
  • Tracks added while Live was playing (with Add Files, Add Folder, drag and drop, a loaded playlist, or from the Remote) did not play: playback stopped after the tracks that were queued when you pressed Play.
  • With DSD output and Allow 48× DSD off, the time and track shown for 48k-family music fell further behind the music with each track. Pause and Play could then repeat music, and Next could restart the playing track.
  • With Device Input (for example from the PGGB Bridge) playing to a UPnP or PAL output, 44.1 kHz music with EQ on dropped short passages to silence now and then.
  • With DSD output to a PAL endpoint, after a seek, a pause or a volume change the track bar, time and title could run several seconds behind the music (about the Network buffer’s length) for the rest of the session, and a later volume change could play those seconds again.
  • Live refused AAC and Opus tracks from a UPnP server, although it played the same files from a folder.
  • A few intact FLAC files stopped early in Live, and Offline refused them as incomplete (“could not read the complete track”).
  • PGGB wrongly reported that “the audio device ran dry” when it paused because the player sending to the PGGB Bridge stopped (Device Input), and on the Mac when you pressed Stop during play.
  • On the Remote, the mark on the playing track in the Library stood still on a computer or phone set to reduce motion (for example with Windows’ Animation effects off). It now moves, as in the app.

Reporting a problem

  • In Settings > Live > Logs and statistics, turn on Log diagnostics, reproduce the problem, then send us the latest pggb_live_….log from the PGGB folder in your home folder (Windows: C:\Users\<you>\PGGB; Mac: your home folder, then PGGB). Turn diagnostics off when you are done.
  • Stealth stops every log, so turn it off before you reproduce a problem.
  • Please include the format and rate of the music you played, your output device, and what happened.
  • For the Library, Remote mode and the Remote, a screenshot and the steps that led to the problem help most.

Thank you for testing.
