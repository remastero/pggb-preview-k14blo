PGGB V9 beta update (9.1.49) for testers

Downloads, what is new since 9.1.48, and notes for testing.

An update to the PGGB V9 beta, version 9.1.49, is ready for testing. Since 9.1.48 it adds Headphone EQ, which applies an EQ preset made for your headphones. The Library gains Composer names and Add field, and DSD tracks now get their DR as they play. Squeezebox playback of 705.6 and 768 kHz files from LMS is fixed, and the other fixes are listed below.

This is a beta. Some parts are still being finished. Please tell us what you see, even small things; Reporting a problem below says how.

Installation

  • Windows: this installer is not signed, so Windows may warn when you start it: choose More info, then Run anyway. On a PC with Smart App Control on, Windows will not start it.
  • Install it over 9.1.48; your settings and Library stay as they are.
  • Mac: use the Apple silicon installer on M1 and later Macs and the Intel installer on Intel Macs. About This Mac in the Apple menu shows which chip yours has.
  • PAL endpoints: keep the ones you have; this update needs no PAL update.

What is new since 9.1.48

Headphone EQ

Headphone EQ (Settings > Personalize Sound > Headphone EQ) is new: choose your headphones and apply an EQ preset made for them, from OPRA, the open directory of headphone EQ presets. PGGB applies it as your parametric EQ.

Details
  • PGGB fetches the presets the first time the page opens and checks once a week for new ones.
  • Headphone EQ is on the Remote too.

The Library

Composer names (Settings > Library > Appearance): show composers as First name Last name or Last name, First name. It changes how composers are shown, not how lists sort.

Details
  • In Customize views, Add field under Browse fields makes a new field from your files' tags (for example a recording location) and adds it to the view, without leaving the view editor.
  • Both are on the Remote too.

DR for DSD tracks

DSD tracks now get their DR (dynamic range) when they play, as PCM tracks do.

Remote mode and the Remote

In Remote mode, the PGGB that shows another computer's PGGB now stays idle itself: it plays nothing, closing its window quits it, and its tray or menu-bar icon is hidden.

Details
  • On the Remote, the waveform shows the app's pointer: a left-right arrow near the playhead, where you can drag it.

Notes for testing

  • The first time the Library opens after the update, it updates its composer and genre lists once, with a progress bar. On a large library this can take a few minutes.
  • Headphone EQ needs an internet connection the first time its page opens, to fetch the presets.
  • Check for updates looks at public releases only, so it does not show this beta update.
  • On the Remote, the play queue has Clear queue, but not yet a single entry's Remove from queue, Move earlier or Move later.

Fixed since 9.1.48

If you saw any of these, they are fixed now:

The 8 fixes
  • In a view, a track whose genre differs from its album's (for example a chamber work in an orchestral box set) was listed under the album's genre. Each track is now listed under its own genre.
  • Opening an album at the end of a view's path showed all of its tracks, including those the view leaves out. It now shows only the view's tracks, with Show the whole album for the rest.
  • An album opened from the Genres page showed all of its tracks, not only those of that genre.
  • Album covers could flash while you scrolled through a view.
  • Three composers with two-word surnames were listed under the wrong letter: Ralph Vaughan Williams, Joly Braga Santos and Josquin des Prez.
  • Tracks tagged Symphony were listed under the genre Symphonic.
  • Installing an update could stop and wait while PGGB had setting changes you had not saved. PGGB now closes for the update and keeps your changes.
  • With the Squeezebox input and LMS, WAV and AIFF files at 705.6 or 768 kHz played slowly and sounded distorted.

Reporting a problem

  • In Settings > Live > Logs and statistics, turn on Log diagnostics, reproduce the problem, then send us the latest pggb_live_….log from the PGGB folder in your home folder (Windows: C:\Users\<you>\PGGB; Mac: your home folder, then PGGB). Turn diagnostics off when you are done.
  • Stealth stops every log, so turn it off before you reproduce a problem.
  • Please include the format and rate of the music you played, your output device, and what happened.
  • For the Library, Headphone EQ, Remote mode and the Remote, a screenshot and the steps that led to the problem help most.

Thank you for testing.
